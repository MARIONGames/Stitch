How this works & running it for real

A WiFi receiver computes Channel State Information (amplitude + phase per OFDM subcarrier) for every packet it decodes from your router. A moving body reshapes the multipath between router and receiver; the waterfall above is that disturbance. A human body is a large reflector, so its motion is coherent across subcarriers and carries a slow breathing rhythm; a pet is small and incoherent; a fan is a fast, narrowband, highly periodic machine. The classifier reads exactly those features.

This page runs on simulated CSI so it works on any phone with no hardware — a browser can't read your WiFi chip's CSI directly. For real capture, the matching Python app reads an ESP32 (ESP-CSI firmware over USB) or Nexmon / Intel 5300 pcap exports, and feeds them through this same detect→classify pipeline.
